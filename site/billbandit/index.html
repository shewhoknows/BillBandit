BillBandit · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

This policy explains how BillBandit, operated by Esha Bhoon, handles information when you use the BillBandit iOS app and related services.

Information we handle

BillBandit handles only the information needed to provide account, group-ledger, synchronization, and support features. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Account information: Apple sign-in identifiers, name, email address or Apple relay address, username, and session information.
  • Optional profile information: preferred name, phone number, avatar, and payment identifier such as a UPI ID.
  • Ledger information: group names, members, expenses, amounts, currencies, dates, categories, notes, splits, balances, settlements, and activity associated with those records.
  • Device and local app data: information stored on your device to keep the app working, including local ledger data and securely stored sign-in credentials.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep your account signed in.
  • Create and synchronize groups, expenses, balances, and settlements across the devices and people you choose to share with.
  • Display profiles and group activity to the members of the relevant group.
  • Protect the service, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, and respond to support or privacy requests.
  • Meet legal obligations when we are required to do so.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising profiles. Information may be processed by the following services when needed to operate BillBandit:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and iCloud/CloudKit services used for account identity and optional friend/profile synchronization.
  • BillBandit backend infrastructure: the app’s API and database infrastructure process account and ledger data so the service can provide authenticated sync.
  • Support providers: information you include in a message to our privacy email is used to answer that request.

We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect the service, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

Sharing inside BillBandit

Group information is visible to the members of that group and to anyone you intentionally invite or add. Please do not add another person’s email address or personal information unless you are authorized to do so.

Retention and deletion

You can start account deletion from Profile → Apple Account → Delete account in the app. This removes your sign-in credentials and personal profile data from the BillBandit backend, deletes your local app data, and removes your public friend profile where possible. Shared ledger amounts may remain for the other members, with your profile and authored text anonymized so their balances stay correct. For questions or help with a request, contact privacy@eshabhoon.com.

Security

BillBandit uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. Sign-in credentials are stored securely on supported devices, and network connections to the service use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s privacy

BillBandit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when BillBandit’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through another reasonable notice where appropriate.

Questions or requests?
Visit BillBandit support or contact Esha Bhoon at privacy@eshabhoon.com.
