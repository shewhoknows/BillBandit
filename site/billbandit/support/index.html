BillBandit · Support

How can we help?

BillBandit keeps shared expenses, balances, and settlement records in sync across the people in your group.

Account and sign-in

Use Profile → Apple Account to reconnect Sign in with Apple or sign out. If the app says it cannot reach BillBandit, check your connection and try again.

Sync and balances

Shared groups use the BillBandit API as the source of truth. The app may keep a short-lived on-device cache for offline recovery; opening the group while online refreshes the latest server revision.

Delete your account

Start from Profile → Apple Account → Delete account. This removes your sign-in credentials and personal profile data, clears local app data, and anonymizes your identity on shared ledger records so the remaining members’ balances continue to work.

Still need help?
Email privacy@eshabhoon.com with the issue, the app version, and the steps that led to it. Please do not include passwords or authentication codes.
